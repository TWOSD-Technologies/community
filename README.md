# 2SD Technologies community

This is the open space for people who build with **2SD Technologies** and **yOGI**, our enterprise AI platform. We share what we learn about running AI in real organisations: private models, governed agents, data platforms and the engineering behind them.

## What happens here

- **[Discussions](https://github.com/TWOSD-Technologies/community/discussions)** cover our articles, ideas and questions. Every leadership and engineering article we publish gets a thread here.
- **Ideas** is where you suggest topics, tools or patterns you'd like us to write about or build.
- **Show and tell** is for sharing what you've built with agents, private models or data platforms.
- **Q&A** is for questions about enterprise AI architecture, governance and delivery.

## Contributing

We welcome contributors: engineers, data scientists, architects and writers.

- Start or join a discussion.
- Propose an article or a talk in **Ideas**.
- Keep it practical and respectful. No sales pitches, and nothing confidential about your employer or clients.
